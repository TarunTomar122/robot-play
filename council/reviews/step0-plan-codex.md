**Novelty — 5.5/10.** The combination of learning curves, physical quirks, disturbances and tool-level ablations is a worthwhile question ([plan:9–18](<repo>/NIGHT-2026-11-16.md:9)). The individual ingredients are close to prior work: [RoboScript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12885) already rehearses in simulation and writes task memory; [GPT-Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19138) studies interaction feedback and context ablations. The table needs evidence and a sharper claim about the specific combination.

**Rigour — 3.0/10.** Simulator predicates and seed-level intervals are good intentions ([plan:44–51](<repo>/NIGHT-2026-11-16.md:44)). There is no sample size, seed allocation, paired evaluation, held-out split, disturbance protocol, or primary comparison. “Matches” needs a prespecified equivalence margin and confidence interval; cheap-model costs must include all practice and memory-generation calls ([plan:7](<repo>/NIGHT-2026-11-16.md:7)).

**Engineering — 2.0/10.** The skills, tests, agent loop and spend meter are proposed, not demonstrated ([plan:59–65](<repo>/NIGHT-2026-11-16.md:59)); the repo currently contains only an empty package initializer alongside the plan and configuration. Unbounded dependency versions ([pyproject.toml:5–18](<repo>/pyproject.toml:5)) do not establish reproducibility. A hard cap needs pre-call reservation, concurrency control, accounting for retries and reconciliation against provider usage.

**Validity — 3.0/10.** The oracle-versus-naive check ([plan:61–62](<repo>/NIGHT-2026-11-16.md:61)) cannot establish that quirks are physical, discoverable from permitted observations, or causally responsible for improvement. Memory may leak test states; successful-trajectory selection and disturbances may also change difficulty. Define stable physical predicates, observable feedback, reachability checks and independent evaluation before interpreting learning curves.

**Clarity — 5.0/10.** The question, conditions and intended order are readable ([plan:3–7](<repo>/NIGHT-2026-11-16.md:3), [plan:31–57](<repo>/NIGHT-2026-11-16.md:31)). But the experiment matrix, timelines and cost ledger are absent. **The full plan is not credibly feasible overnight or within $90 Anthropic/$45 OpenAI as specified:** five tasks, two robots, four memories, multiple models and disturbances are proposed before the core simulator exists, with no measured episode cost or runtime. A cap limits spending; it does not prove the experiment fits.
