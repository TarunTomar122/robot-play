# Firm grip on `stack`: how often Haiku sets it, and what follows

Held-out `stack` episodes of Claude Haiku 5.5 on the corrected simulator (run seeds 20 to 29, 4 layouts per run, 40 per arm). An episode counts as *firm* if any call in it passes `grip_level` firm; *turned hand only* means a 45 degree `yaw_offset_deg` without a firm grip; *neither* means the default grip with no turn. In every turn-only episode the model also set `speed` slow (some also a grasp height of 0.5 or placed the cube by hand with `move_to` and `open_gripper`), a combination the one-setting-at-a-time settings audit did not test.
